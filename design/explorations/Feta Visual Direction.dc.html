3Fresh start · minimal, classy, international

What I took from research on current premium apps: one decision per screen, typography doing most of the work, a restrained palette with one accent, and more detail only when you ask for it (progressive disclosure). So these three drop the outlines, orbs, floating cards and chip clutter. Each pick now fills the screen like a magazine page. Haze is kept to a quiet tint where the photo meets the page.

Each direction shows two screens: Picks and Taste reveal. The content is international (Lisbon, prices as $$). The Google Maps line stays on its own neutral band in all of them.
