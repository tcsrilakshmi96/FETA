6Colourways of 5a · each named after a place

Same layout and motion, five palettes. The bottom bar is now ✕ · Details · ♥. Tapping ✕ or ♥ no longer opens the details. The details sheet now sits above the bar, so Close is always within reach.

6aMarrakech · espresso, terracotta, apricot
Riad walls at sunset. The warmest of the five, good with food photos and leather and linen in fashion.
6bAmalfi · deep olive, lemon, cream
Lemon groves and long lunches. Sunny without being loud, with a summer-holiday feel.
6cParis · bordeaux, rose, blush
Wine bars and ateliers. The most fashion-led of the five; works well for nightlife and the future Style picks.
6dAegean · deep sea, coral, shell
Harbour at dusk. Coral against deep teal feels like travel and adventure.
6eChampagne Noir · black, champagne gold, ivory
A hotel bar in Milan. The quietest and most premium; lets the photos carry the colour.
