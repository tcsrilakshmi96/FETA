# Feta — project guide for Claude Code

Feta (working name: Fashion, Experience, Travel, All in one) is a personal AI lifestyle companion that learns a user's taste and decides for them: 3 confident picks for food and drinks, and 3 for experiences, each with a reason.

The full v1 spec (features, screens, onboarding questions, two end-to-end journeys) lives in the founder's doc "Feta v1 Feature List". Ask the founder to paste a section when you need detail.

The approved designs are in `design/`. Start with `design/README.md` (screens, rules, tokens, motion) and `design/DATA_CONTRACT.md` (data shapes). `design/prototype/Feta App v2.dc.html` is the visual source of truth; `design/explorations/` is earlier rounds for context only (they include dropped ideas such as taste types). Screenshots of every screen and state are in `design/screenshots/light/` and `design/screenshots/dark/`. Prototype content (Lisbon, Tokyo, $ prices, Unsplash photos) is placeholder: use Indian cities and ₹ for the friends test. This file and `design/` were checked against each other on 10 Nov 2026 and agree. If you still find a conflict, the design wins for look, copy and flow; this file wins for data, privacy and the rules below. Tell the founder what you found.

## v1 scope (friends test, India, Nov–Dec 2026)
- Mobile web app opened from a link. Phone-first, works on slow connections. Light and dark mode: Auto follows the phone, and the user can choose Auto / Light / Dark in You (tokens in `design/README.md`).
- Flow: Welcome (18+ check) → Google sign-in and consent → city → 6 question screens (diet, distance, spend per person, company, drinks, interests) → 6 swipe cards → "Here's what I picked up" → location → picks. Aim for first picks in about 90 seconds.
- Sign-in comes right after Welcome (founder's decision): "Continue with Google", then consent toggles. "Not now" skips it and the user can sign in later from You. The Welcome screen must not say "No sign-up".
- Consent toggles: "Use my location" and "Show my reviews to others" are both off by default (founder's decision, 10 Nov 2026; this overrides the design, where location is on). Tapping "Use my location" on the Location screen turns the location toggle on. Both can be changed any time in You. The browser's own location prompt still appears only when the user taps "Use my location".
- Diet options: Everything, Vegetarian, Vegetarian + eggs, Jain, Vegan, Pescatarian, plus a Halal only toggle (Jain added by founder's decision).
- Spend per person is shown in the local currency (founder's decision). It comes from the chosen city; if none is chosen yet, it is guessed from the phone's time zone (no permission, not stored; unknown zones fall back to USD). The answer is stored as a level (1–5 or "depends") plus its currency code, so it can be matched to price ranges in another city. Bands live in `lib/currency.ts`; India: under ₹300, ₹300–500, ₹500–1,500, ₹1,500–3,000, ₹3,000+.
- No taste types or labels on users. The profile is plain, editable lines, and can hold notes tied to an occasion or a trip.
- Picks home: "Tonight" and "This weekend" tabs, a city pill, and a category switch: "Food & drinks" (EAT and DRINK cards) and "Experiences" (DO and EVENT cards), plus a muted "Style · soon" that opens the Style teaser. Each category has exactly 3 picks, shown one card at a time, and the two are never mixed on this screen (founder's decision). The title follows both switches ("Your 3 to eat tonight", "Your 3 to do this weekend"). The last category is remembered, each category keeps its own progress and states, and "Tweak these" applies to the category in view. Chat replies may still mix categories. There is no "Who's with you?" chip (founder's decision): company comes from the onboarding answer, the Tweak sheet ("Better for groups") and chat.
- Every pick has a "Because you…" line, matched chips, honest wording ("Worth a detour" / "Good if you're nearby" / "Skip unless…"), and an action row: Book on District / Swiggy Dineout / BookMyShow (link out), Directions, Save, Share to WhatsApp.
- Chat ("Ask Feta"): plan for a destination (India or abroad), change choices, ask "why this one?". What it learns is added to the profile straight away and shown in an "ADDED TO YOUR PROFILE" card with Undo. When the reply is tied to a trip, the card also has an "Only for this trip" toggle (off by default). Trip-only lines are never used for everyday picks and appear in their own group in the profile, with "Use these every day instead".
- Location in two steps (founder's decision): a "Which city?" screen right after sign-in (no location popup; sets currency and coverage, cities not covered say "Not yet" and suggest the nearest covered ones), then the exact-location screen just before the picks ("Near me now": one-time location while the app is open, never stored as a trail, with "Pick a city instead"). Cities live in `lib/cities.ts`; the covered list is a placeholder until the founder confirms it.
- After an outing: Loved it / It was fine / Not for me, optional review and up to 3 photos, with a "Show my review to others" toggle off by default. Photos and public reviews need founder approval on an admin screen.
- Feta Favourite badge: at least 3 ratings and at least 70% loved.
- Style and shopping: a "coming soon" card and tab with a "Tell me when" button that counts interest. No workflow.
- Sign-in provider for the friends test: Google sign-in through Supabase Auth (founder's decision, 10 Nov 2026: free, one tap for most Indian users). This replaces the design's phone number and 6-digit code. Phone codes (Firebase Phone Auth, which handles India's SMS DLT registration) may come back after the trial.
- Weekend plan: drafted by the app, sent by the founder on WhatsApp. No web push in v1. The WhatsApp number is optional and asked separately, not at sign-in: a "Want your weekend plan on WhatsApp?" prompt with a number field, on My lists (founder's decision). It is stored only after the user adds it, used only for the weekend plan, and removable in You.
- A small feedback button on every screen.
- Not in v1: group mode, full travel mode, native app, background location, in-app booking, allergy filter.

## Changes after the design (founder's decisions, 10 Nov 2026)
These came from a 12-persona test of the flow. They override `design/` wherever the two differ. Keep the design's look and components; change only what is listed.
- Taste swipes: 6 cards, not 10. Counter and progress read "n / 6". Add a visible "Skip" that goes straight to "Here's what I picked up". Keep one card each for: a classic local breakfast, a spicy regional meal, hole-in-the-wall street food, a quiet cafe, a lively night out (swap for a non-alcohol option when the user chose "No alcohol"), and a hands-on or outdoor experience. Welcome copy becomes "A few quick questions, six swipes, then your picks."
- "New to you" rule for picks: the first set of 3 in each category should feel like a find, not the place everyone already knows. At most 1 of the 3 may be a widely known, very popular place. Never show a place the user has saved, marked Been, or skipped. Tell the AI this in the picks prompt; keep diet, budget, distance, alcohol and opening hours as hard filters in code.
- Spend question: add "Under ₹300" as the lowest option (then ₹300–500, ₹500–1,500, ₹1,500–3,000, ₹3,000+, Depends on the day).
- Company question: add "Family with kids". When chosen, picks prefer family-friendly places and the Tweak sheet gains "Good with kids".
- Check-in: users whose diet is Jain, vegan or halal-only see one extra optional tick after "Loved it" or "It was fine": "It worked for my diet (Jain / vegan / halal)". On pick details, show the count as "3 Feta users who eat Jain said it worked for them. Still check with staff." Show it only at 2 or more confirmations. This never replaces the "not confirmed, check with staff" rule and must never say or imply the food is safe.
- Not changed: sign-in still comes right after Welcome with "Not now". The founder is rethinking this separately; do not move it without her say-so.

## Proposed stack (confirm with the founder before installing)
- Next.js web app, hosted on Vercel.
- Supabase for the database, Google sign-in, file storage (review photos) and row-level security.
- AI split (founder's decision, 10 Nov 2026): Venice knows the person, Gemini knows the places.
  - Venice AI (OpenAI-compatible API, founder's credits) for chat, profile updates (with Undo / Only for this trip), turning what the user says into a structured search request, and turning reviews into tags. Venice never receives Google Maps or Places content. Use a mainstream model, not an "uncensored" one.
  - Gemini API with Grounding with Google Maps finds places, chooses the 3 picks per category from those that pass the hard filters, and writes the "Because you…" line and verdict. Gemini receives only a short taste summary built from Feta's own data. Show each pick's Google Maps source right below it, as Google requires.
  - "Why this one?" in chat is answered from the reason Gemini already wrote.
- Google Places API (New) for the hard-filter checks (open now, hours, price level, diet and alcohol signals, distance) and live open-now status on the picks shown.
- Cost guards: a ₹1,000 monthly budget alert in Google Cloud, a daily search limit enforced in Feta, and picks reused per person for a few hours instead of re-searching on every open.
- Web search (Venice or Gemini with Google Search) for this week's events, linking out to the event page.
- Foursquare open places data or Overture Maps as a stored base list of places.
- PostHog for usage analytics.

## How picks work (built 10 Nov 2026)
- Location screen before picks: "Use my location" (one-time, kept only in the tab's session, never stored; turns location consent on) or an area in the chosen city (areas in `lib/cities.ts`).
- `/api/picks` (server only, keys never reach phones), one Google Places search per new set. Food & drinks: Nearby Search for every restaurant, café, bakery and bar within the user's distance (founder: "with location on, everything near me should show up"), most popular first, with "Show me 3 more" skipping what was already shown (closest-first surfaced juice and dessert counters as dinner). Experiences: Text Search with a phrase from the user's interests and swipes, rotating on "Show me 3 more", never showing places that are mainly for eating or drinking. Then hard filters in code (`lib/server/picks.ts`) → Gemini 3.5 Flash ranks up to 8 → code keeps 3 with at most 1 very popular place (3,000+ Google reviews), relaxing that only rather than show fewer than 3 → `pick_sets` stores place IDs and Feta's own text only. An empty result says which filter removed the places.
- Hard-filter details: places with no price on Google show only for spend levels 1–3 (up to ₹1,500) and "Depends on the day", not for ₹1,500–3,000 or ₹3,000+ (founder's decision); vegetarian diets need Google's "serves vegetarian food" = yes (food and drinks only); "No alcohol" drops bars, pubs and clubs; Tonight needs open now; This weekend needs Sat or Sun hours; closed businesses, saved, skipped and "been" places are never shown. Halal can't be checked from Google data yet: the option stays (founder's decision, useful outside India), is not used as a filter, and food picks for halal users carry the chip "Halal · not confirmed, check with staff". Add a real halal source before using it as a filter.
- Always 3 when possible: Food & drinks searches everything popular nearby plus a search built from the user's tweaks and "Yes, me" swipes (so picks follow their taste); Experiences searches two interest phrases. If fewer than needed pass the hard filters, one wider search (2× distance, shown as "worth a short ride"). A reused set that has lost places is topped back up to 3.
- Tweaks (`TWEAKS` in `lib/server/picks.ts`): Cheaper (spend one level down), Closer (distance one step down, to 1 km) and Open later (still open at 11 pm) change the hard filters; Quieter, Outdoors, Better for groups, Good with kids and free text steer the search words and Gemini. Tweaked sets always search fresh and are stored apart from the everyday set.
- Picks are reused for 3 hours (Tonight) or 12 hours (weekend), re-checked live with Google each time. Cost limits: `PICKS_DAILY_LIMIT` (30 new searches a day across everyone) and `PICKS_USER_DAILY_LIMIT` (6 per person), changeable in Vercel (founder confirmed 30 and 6 for the friends test).
- Matched chips are written by code from verified facts, never by the AI. Jain and vegan chips say "not confirmed, check with staff". Shares to WhatsApp never include diet or chips.
- Experiences = venues + this week's events (founder's decision: A+B). Events: Gemini 3.5 Flash with Grounding with Google Search, via the Interactions API (`lib/server/events.ts`; generateContent with a JSON schema returned no search sources, so it may invent events). Sent: the area name (never coordinates) and the taste summary; at most 2 searches, low thinking, 18 s cap, then places only. Code checks each event: a real https listing link (no redirects), the right date and time in the city's time zone, the budget (unknown price follows the no-price rule), and distance via the venue found on Google Maps (cheaper "Pro" fields). At most 2 events per set, then venues to make 3; venue ranking runs alongside the event search. Event cards are EVENT, link out ("Tickets on BookMyShow"), and show Google's Search Suggestions chip unaltered where food cards show the Google Maps line (Google requires it). Event listings are kept with the set; for an event, place_id is "event:<page address>".
- Not built yet: Tweak sheet, full Details screen, Lists, Ask, You, Style, check-in, feedback sheet.

## Rules that must not be broken
- Diet, budget, distance, alcohol and opening hours are hard filters enforced in code, never left to the AI.
- Never claim food is safe for an allergy or diet. Jain and vegan matches say "not confirmed, check with staff". Use neutral diet labels; never "pure veg".
- Google data: store only Google place IDs. Show live Google data on its own labelled line, e.g. "Open now · checked just now · Google Maps", with the words "Google Maps" unchanged. Never use Google content to train or test AI models.
- Do not scrape Zomato, District, Swiggy, BookMyShow or Meetup. Link out to them.
- Published lists (Condé Nast Traveller, 50 Best, Culinary Culture, 30 Best Bars) may be used only as "Featured in" facts with links. Never copy their text, photos or logos.
- Privacy: 18+ check, itemised consent with a separate location toggle, a consent log, one-tap "delete my data", a grievance email. Nothing public by default. Diet never appears on anything shareable.
- Every place, event and user record carries city, country, time zone and currency, so new cities are a data job, not a rebuild.

## Metrics to build in
