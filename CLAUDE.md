# Feta — project guide for Claude Code

Feta (working name: Fashion, Experience, Travel, All in one) is a personal AI lifestyle companion that learns a user's taste and decides for them: 3 confident picks for food and drinks, and 3 for experiences, each with a reason.

The full v1 spec (features, screens, onboarding questions, two end-to-end journeys) lives in the founder's doc "Feta v1 Feature List". Ask the founder to paste a section when you need detail.

The approved designs are in `design/`. Start with `design/README.md` (screens, rules, tokens, motion) and `design/DATA_CONTRACT.md` (data shapes). `design/prototype/Feta App v2.dc.html` is the visual source of truth; `design/explorations/` is earlier rounds for context only (they include dropped ideas such as taste types). Screenshots of every screen and state are in `design/screenshots/light/` and `design/screenshots/dark/`. Prototype content (Lisbon, Tokyo, $ prices, Unsplash photos) is placeholder: real content comes from the user's city and currency (Feta is international from v1). This file and `design/` were checked against each other on 10 Nov 2026 and agree. If you still find a conflict, the design wins for look, copy and flow; this file wins for data, privacy and the rules below. Tell the founder what you found.

## v1 scope (alpha test, international, Nov–Dec 2026)
- International from v1 (founder's decision, 11 Nov 2026; overrides the earlier India-only city list): many alpha testers live abroad. Any city in the world, local currency, km or miles, local booking links and events. Launch cities to test before the alpha: Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Berlin, Frankfurt, Cologne, Koblenz, Prague, Lisbon, Paris, Metz, London, Dubai, Singapore, Hong Kong, Seoul, Melbourne, San Francisco, New York, Houston, Chicago.
- Mobile web app opened from a link. Phone-first, works on slow connections. Light and dark mode: Auto follows the phone, and the user can choose Auto / Light / Dark in You (tokens in `design/README.md`).
- Flow: Welcome (18+ check) → Google sign-in and consent → city → 6 question screens (diet, distance, spend per person, company, drinks, interests) → 6 swipe cards → "Here's what I picked up" → location → picks. Aim for first picks in about 90 seconds.
- Sign-in comes right after Welcome (founder's decision): "Continue with Google", then consent toggles. "Not now" skips it and the user can sign in later from You. The Welcome screen must not say "No sign-up".
- Consent toggles: "Use my location" and "Show my reviews to others" are both off by default (founder's decision, 10 Nov 2026; this overrides the design, where location is on). Tapping "Use my location" on the Location screen turns the location toggle on. Both can be changed any time in You. The browser's own location prompt still appears only when the user taps "Use my location".
- Diet options: Everything, Vegetarian, Vegetarian + eggs, Jain, Vegan, Pescatarian, plus a Halal only toggle (Jain added by founder's decision).
- Spend per person is shown in the local currency (founder's decision). It comes from the chosen city; if none is chosen yet, it is guessed from the phone's time zone (no permission, not stored; unknown zones fall back to USD). The answer is stored as a level (1–5 or "depends") plus its currency code; the level travels (₹500–1,500 at home means the same kind of place, at local prices, abroad). Bands live in `lib/currency.ts`: hand-set for INR, USD, CAD, GBP, EUR, CHF, CZK, AED, SGD, HKD, THB, AUD, NZD, JPY, KRW; about 40 more scaled from the US bands by a rough rate; anything else falls back to US dollars. India: under ₹300, ₹300–500, ₹500–1,500, ₹1,500–3,000, ₹3,000+. Distances are km, or miles in the US and UK (`formatDistance` in `lib/cities.ts`; the limits stay 1, 2, 5 km).
- No taste types or labels on users. The profile is plain, editable lines, and can hold notes tied to an occasion or a trip.
- Picks home: "Tonight" and "This weekend" tabs, a city pill, and a category switch: "Food & drinks" (EAT and DRINK cards) and "Experiences" (DO and EVENT cards), plus a muted "Style · soon" that opens the Style teaser. Each category has exactly 3 picks, shown one card at a time, and the two are never mixed on this screen (founder's decision). The title follows both switches ("Your 3 to eat tonight", "Your 3 to do this weekend"). The last category is remembered, each category keeps its own progress and states, and "Tweak these" applies to the category in view. Chat replies may still mix categories. There is no "Who's with you?" chip (founder's decision): company comes from the onboarding answer, the Tweak sheet ("Better for groups") and chat.
- Every pick has a "Because you…" line, matched chips, honest wording ("Worth a detour" / "Good if you're nearby" / "Skip unless…"), and an action row: the booking or map button, Directions, Save, Send to the group. Booking brands only where they operate (founder's decision): Book on District for tables and BookMyShow / District for events in India only; elsewhere "Open in Google Maps" leads, with the venue's own website, and events link to their ticket page. South Korea: "Open in Naver Map" (a search link by place name) leads, Google Maps second. Mainland China is not supported in v1: choosing a mainland city says so plainly and offers chat-only ideas marked less reliable; Hong Kong and Macau work as normal.
- Chat ("Ask Feta"): plan for a destination (India or abroad), change choices, ask "why this one?". What it learns is added to the profile straight away and shown in an "ADDED TO YOUR PROFILE" card with Undo. When the reply is tied to a trip, the card also has an "Only for this trip" toggle (off by default). Trip-only lines are never used for everyday picks and appear in their own group in the profile, with "Use these every day instead".
- Location in two steps (founder's decision): a "Which city?" screen right after sign-in where the user types any city in the world (no location popup, no "Not yet"; sets currency, units and time zone), then the exact-location screen just before the picks: "Use my location" (one-time, never stored as a trail; the city follows the phone), or a typed neighbourhood or landmark, or "Anywhere in <city>". Cities come from GeoNames (`data/cities.json`, every city of 15,000+ people with English alternative names, built by `scripts/build-cities.mjs`, CC BY 4.0, credited on the privacy page), searched on Feta's server (`lib/server/cities.ts`, `/api/cities`): free, and storable, unlike Google answers. City ids are "gn:<GeoNames id>"; Goa and Bali are added by hand.
- After an outing: Loved it / It was fine / Not for me, optional review and up to 3 photos, with a "Show my review to others" toggle off by default. Photos and public reviews need founder approval on an admin screen.
- Feta Favourite badge: at least 3 ratings and at least 70% loved.
- Style and shopping: a "coming soon" card and tab with a "Tell me when" button that counts interest. No workflow.
- Sign-in provider for the friends test: Google sign-in through Supabase Auth (founder's decision, 10 Nov 2026: free, one tap for most users). This replaces the design's phone number and 6-digit code. Phone codes (Firebase Phone Auth, which handles India's SMS DLT registration) may come back after the trial.
- Weekend plan: drafted by the app, sent by the founder on WhatsApp. No web push in v1. The WhatsApp number is optional and asked separately, not at sign-in: a "Want your weekend plan on WhatsApp?" prompt with a number field, on My lists (founder's decision). It is stored only after the user adds it, used only for the weekend plan, and removable in You.
- A small feedback button on every screen.
- Not in v1: group mode, full travel mode, native app, background location, in-app booking, allergy filter.

## Changes after the design (founder's decisions, 10 Nov 2026)
These came from a 12-persona test of the flow. They override `design/` wherever the two differ. Keep the design's look and components; change only what is listed.
- Taste swipes: 6 cards, not 10. Counter and progress read "n / 6". Add a visible "Skip" that goes straight to "Here's what I picked up". Keep one card each for: a classic local breakfast, a spicy regional meal, hole-in-the-wall street food, a quiet cafe, a lively night out (swap for a non-alcohol option when the user chose "No alcohol"), and a hands-on or outdoor experience. Welcome copy becomes "A few quick questions, six swipes, then your picks."
- "New to you" rule for picks: the first set of 3 in each category should feel like a find, not the place everyone already knows. At most 1 of the 3 may be a widely known, very popular place. Never show a place the user has saved, marked Been, or skipped. Tell the AI this in the picks prompt; keep diet, budget, distance, alcohol and opening hours as hard filters in code.
- Spend question: add "Under ₹300" as the lowest option (then ₹300–500, ₹500–1,500, ₹1,500–3,000, ₹3,000+, Depends on the day).
- Company question: add "Family with kids". When chosen, picks prefer family-friendly places and the Tweak sheet gains "Good with kids".
- Check-in: users whose diet is Jain, vegan or halal-only see one extra optional tick after "Loved it" or "It was fine": "It worked for my diet (Jain / vegan / halal)". On pick details, show the count as "3 Feta users who eat Jain said it worked for them. Still check with staff." Show it only at 2 or more confirmations. This never replaces the "not confirmed, check with staff" rule and must never say or imply the food is safe.
- Not changed: sign-in still comes right after Welcome with "Not now". The founder is rethinking this separately; do not move it without her say-so.

## Proposed stack (confirm with the founder before installing)
- Next.js web app, hosted on Vercel.
- Supabase for the database, Google sign-in, file storage (review photos) and row-level security.
- AI split (founder's decision, 10 Nov 2026): Venice knows the person, Gemini knows the places.
  - Venice AI (OpenAI-compatible API, founder's credits) for chat, profile updates (with Undo / Only for this trip), turning what the user says into a structured search request, and turning reviews into tags. Venice never receives Google Maps or Places content. Use a mainstream model, not an "uncensored" one.
  - Gemini API with Grounding with Google Maps finds places, chooses the 3 picks per category from those that pass the hard filters, and writes the "Because you…" line and verdict. Gemini receives only a short taste summary built from Feta's own data. Show each pick's Google Maps source right below it, as Google requires.
  - "Why this one?" in chat is answered from the reason Gemini already wrote.
- Google Places API (New) for the hard-filter checks (open now, hours, price level, diet and alcohol signals, distance) and live open-now status on the picks shown.
- Cost guards: a ₹1,000 monthly budget alert in Google Cloud, a daily search limit enforced in Feta, and picks reused per person for a few hours instead of re-searching on every open.
- Web search (Venice or Gemini with Google Search) for this week's events, linking out to the event page.
- Foursquare open places data or Overture Maps as a stored base list of places.
- PostHog for usage analytics.

## How picks work (built 10 Nov 2026)
- Location screen before picks: "Use my location" (one-time, kept only in the tab's session, never stored; turns location consent on; Feta's own city list says which city the point is in), a typed area (Google Places Autocomplete near the city, then the chosen place's location with the cheapest "Essentials" fields; `/api/area`; the phone remembers the area for up to 30 days, as Google allows for a location), or "Anywhere in <city>". Pick sets are keyed by place ID or rounded coordinates, never Google text.
- `/api/picks` (server only, keys never reach phones), one Google Places search per new set. Food & drinks: Nearby Search for every restaurant, café, bakery and bar within the user's distance (founder: "with location on, everything near me should show up"), most popular first, with "Show me 3 more" skipping what was already shown (closest-first surfaced juice and dessert counters as dinner). Experiences: Text Search with a phrase from the user's interests and swipes, rotating on "Show me 3 more", never showing places that are mainly for eating or drinking. Then hard filters in code (`lib/server/picks.ts`) → Gemini 3.5 Flash ranks up to 8 → code keeps 3 with at most 1 very popular place (3,000+ Google reviews), relaxing that only rather than show fewer than 3 → `pick_sets` stores place IDs and Feta's own text only. An empty result says which filter removed the places.
- Hard-filter details: places with no price on Google show only for spend levels 1–3 (up to ₹1,500) and "Depends on the day", not for ₹1,500–3,000 or ₹3,000+ (founder's decision); vegetarian diets need Google's "serves vegetarian food" = yes (food and drinks only); "No alcohol" drops bars, pubs and clubs; Tonight needs open now; This weekend needs Sat or Sun hours; closed businesses, saved, skipped and "been" places are never shown. Halal can't be checked from Google data yet: the option stays (founder's decision, useful outside India), is not used as a filter, and food picks for halal users carry the chip "Halal · not confirmed, check with staff". Add a real halal source before using it as a filter.
- Always 3 when possible: Food & drinks searches everything popular nearby plus a search built from the user's tweaks and "Yes, me" swipes (so picks follow their taste); Experiences searches two interest phrases. If fewer than needed pass the hard filters, one wider search (2× distance, shown as "worth a short ride"). A reused set that has lost places is topped back up to 3.
- Tweaks (`TWEAKS` in `lib/server/picks.ts`): Cheaper (spend one level down), Closer (distance one step down, to 1 km) and Open later (still open at 11 pm) change the hard filters; Quieter, Outdoors, Better for groups, Good with kids and free text steer the search words and Gemini. Tweaked sets always search fresh and are stored apart from the everyday set.
- Picks are reused for 3 hours (Tonight) or 12 hours (weekend), re-checked live with Google each time. Cost limits (founder's decision, 11 Nov 2026, for about 50 alpha testers): `PICKS_DAILY_LIMIT` (new searches a day across everyone, default 600) and `PICKS_USER_DAILY_LIMIT` (per person, default 20; each category counts once), plus `DAILY_BUDGET_INR` (default 170, about ₹5,000 a month): when today's estimated spend reaches it, new searches pause until midnight India time and people see their latest picks. All changeable in Vercel. Every Google and Gemini call is counted per request (`lib/server/costs.ts`, `track` and `withCosts`) into `api_costs` with Google's standard list price; `spend_today_inr()` totals today, and the Monday n8n report gets spend by kind and testers per country. India pricing (about 70% cheaper) needs mostly-India usage, so plan on standard prices. Picks, chat, lists and details routes set `maxDuration` (60 s; details 30 s).
- Matched chips are written by code from verified facts, never by the AI. Jain and vegan chips say "not confirmed, check with staff". Shares to WhatsApp never include diet or chips.
- Experiences = venues + this week's events (founder's decision: A+B). Events: Gemini 3.5 Flash with Grounding with Google Search, via the Interactions API (`lib/server/events.ts`; generateContent with a JSON schema returned no search sources, so it may invent events). Sent: the area name (never coordinates) and the taste summary; at most 2 searches, low thinking, 18 s cap, then places only. Code checks each event: a real https listing link (no redirects), the right date and time in the city's time zone, the budget (unknown price follows the no-price rule), and distance via the venue found on Google Maps (cheaper "Pro" fields). At most 2 events per set, then venues to make 3; venue ranking runs alongside the event search. Event cards are EVENT, link out ("Tickets on BookMyShow"), and show Google's Search Suggestions chip unaltered where food cards show the Google Maps line (Google requires it). Event listings are kept with the set; for an event, place_id is "event:<page address>".
- Lists (`/lists`, `/api/lists`): Want to try (saved) and Been, from `pick_actions` (place IDs only; names and photos fetched live, photos only for the top 8). Each place has a private note; "I've been" moves it to Been; Remove deletes the row. The optional WhatsApp number for the weekend plan lives at the bottom of Lists (logged in `consent_log` as "whatsapp").
- You (`/you`): everyday profile lines (Remove marks `removed_at`), trip-only groups with "Use these every day instead", Change my answers / City (return to You), Continue with Google for anonymous users (returns to You; if that Google account already has a Feta account, it signs in to it without deleting this phone's anonymous data), Appearance Auto / Light / Dark (localStorage `feta.theme`, applied before paint), location and public-review consent toggles (logged), privacy policy, and Delete my data with inline confirm (full reload after).
- Ask Feta (`/ask`, `/api/ask`), opened from the cheese: Venice (`claude-haiku-5-5`, a mainstream model, JSON replies, `lib/server/venice.ts`) reads the chat (last 12 messages, ordered by id) and a taste summary, and returns a short reply, up to 3 lines to remember, an optional trip, an optional search, and "why". Learned lines go straight into `profile_lines` (source chat) and show in an ADDED TO YOUR PROFILE card with Undo and, for trips, "Only for this trip" (sets `trip`). A search runs through the same picks engine (`lib/server/engine.ts`, shared with `/api/picks`, called directly, not over HTTP): any city in the world by name (Feta's own list), or a neighbourhood, landmark or region found on Google Maps and placed in its nearest city (an area in the person's own city, otherwise the whole place as a trip). Mainland China gets ideas from Venice's own knowledge, marked less reliable, and no search. The person's own words lead the place search. "Why this one?" answers from Feta's stored reasons without searching. Chat history keeps text, place IDs and Feta's words only.
- Feedback pill (`components/Feedback.tsx`, in the layout on every screen): message, optional screenshot of the screen behind (html-to-image, small JPEG, the sheet itself left out), saved to the `feedback` table (insert-only for users; the founder reads it in Supabase).
- Animations: screen style files (`*.module.css`) refer to shared keyframes through `var(--kf-in | --kf-up | --kf-kb | --kf-pop | --kf-bar)` from `globals.css`, because Next.js renames animation names inside module files (a plain `fa-pop` there silently does nothing). Keyframes a module defines itself can be named directly.
- Verdicts are honest by default: "Good if you're nearby" is the normal verdict, "Worth a detour" at most once per set (enforced in code across events and places), "Skip unless…" for compromises. Reasons must fit the place and the time (no breakfast reasons for dinner). EAT/DRINK comes from the place's main type only. Shops (liquor stores, supermarkets…) are never picks. Photos prefer visitor uploads over the venue's own (often adverts).
- Picks home loads the other category in the background once the first has loaded, so switching is instant; event searches give up after 12 s.
- Details (`components/PickDetails.tsx`, shared by Picks and Ask chat cards): photo, verdict, Google line or Search chip, why it's yours, chips, and What to order / Highlights from Gemini with Grounding with Google Maps (`/api/details`, read live, never stored, Maps sources shown right after). Share to WhatsApp sends a Feta link, `/p/<place id>`, a public page with the place (live from Google), a Google Maps link and "Get your own 3 picks"; never diet, chips or reasons.
- Learning: "Here's what I picked up" saves every line it can (all yes-swipes, distance, spend, company, interests, drinks, diet) and shows the first 5. Tweaks add removable lines ("Likes quieter places", source "tweaks"). Saves keep Feta's reason (`pick_actions.because`); recent saved reasons and skip reasons go into the taste summary Gemini sees.
- Style (`/style`): coming-soon teaser with the design's blurred collage; "Tell me when it's ready" adds a row to `style_interest`.
- Feta picks (founder's own places): `founder_places` (place ID, kind, her note, city fields), managed on `/admin` (only emails in `admins`; `is_admin()`). The engine checks her places for the city and category live against the person's hard filters; one that passes always takes a spot in the 3, with a FETA PICK badge and her note. An Instagram import is planned once the export arrives.
- Verdicts: the weakest pick of a full set says "Skip unless…" from a checkable fact (closes within the hour, at the edge of the distance, very popular); Gemini's "skipUnless" is at most 7 words.
- Events are searched for the area (not one person's taste) and shared in `events_cache` for 3 h (tonight) or 12 h (weekend); `personalizeEvents` then picks up to 2 and writes reasons per person (~2 s). Picks home loads both categories at once.
- Plan my evening (`lib/plan.ts`, `components/PlanSheet.tsx`): pairs a food or drink pick with an experience (or a DRINK) within 3 km, dinner-first or activity-first, times checked against weekly opening hours for the planned day and event start times. Built in code from the picks on screen.
- Send to the group (`components/SendToGroup.tsx`, `/s/<slug>`): a pick or plan as a Feta link in `shares` (place IDs, event links, times, Feta-pick notes; never diet or the personal reason). Friends vote I'm in / Not this time with an optional name, no sign-up (`cast_vote`, a browser id); anyone with the link sees the tally. Details' Share button is now Send to the group.
- Cost savings (11 Nov 2026): Google's terms allow storing only place IDs, so nothing from Google is cached on the server or shared between people. Instead the phone keeps picks in memory for up to 20 minutes while the app is open (going to Lists and back makes no new Google calls; the line then says "checked N min ago"), and keeps "What to order" per place for an hour (one request even on a double tap). Details sends the pick's name and spot, so it skips a paid Place Details lookup. Testing: one short run per change, every test account deleted after.
- Events abroad: the search names the city and country, uses the city's local date and time, and prefers that country's ticket sites (`LOCAL_TICKETS` in `lib/server/events.ts`; Indian sites only in India).
- Small cities (founder's decision): never pad with weak places. Only places Gemini rated a real fit fill the set, so a small city (Koblenz, Metz) may show 2, with a banner that says so and "worth a short ride" when they are further out; a "Skip unless" place fills in only to reach 2. A set of 2 stays 2 while both still fit (no top-up search). If Gemini fails, the filtered places are used in order.
- Swipe cards are worded to work anywhere ("A classic local breakfast", "Fiery, spicy food"…); the three food photos are South Asian for South Asian cities and international elsewhere (`lib/swipes.ts`).
- WhatsApp number: a country-code picker, defaulting to the city's country; stored as "+<code><number>" (`lib/phone.ts`).
- Privacy for users abroad (flagged, not built; founder to decide with a lawyer): EU/UK users have a right to a copy of their data ("Download my data" would be needed); the policy must say data is stored in Mumbai, India, and list processors (Supabase, Vercel, Google, Venice); analytics such as PostHog would need a consent banner in the EU/UK; an EU representative may be needed. Location stays opt-in and unstored everywhere.
- Not built yet: check-in and reviews, admin review approval, weekend WhatsApp plan drafting, Instagram import.

## Rules that must not be broken
- Diet, budget, distance, alcohol and opening hours are hard filters enforced in code, never left to the AI.
- Never claim food is safe for an allergy or diet. Jain and vegan matches say "not confirmed, check with staff". Use neutral diet labels; never "pure veg".
- Google data: store only Google place IDs. Show live Google data on its own labelled line, e.g. "Open now · checked just now · Google Maps", with the words "Google Maps" unchanged. Never use Google content to train or test AI models.
- Do not scrape Zomato, District, Swiggy, BookMyShow or Meetup. Link out to them.
- Published lists (Condé Nast Traveller, 50 Best, Culinary Culture, 30 Best Bars) may be used only as "Featured in" facts with links. Never copy their text, photos or logos.
- Privacy: 18+ check, itemised consent with a separate location toggle, a consent log, one-tap "delete my data", a grievance email. Nothing public by default. Diet never appears on anything shareable.
- Every place, event and user record carries city, country, time zone and currency, so new cities are a data job, not a rebuild.

## Metrics to build in
