# Feta — project guide for Claude Code

Feta (working name: Fashion, Experience, Travel, All in one) is a personal AI lifestyle companion that learns a user's taste and decides for them: up to 8 picks for food and drinks, and 8 for experiences, best first, each with a reason.

The full v1 spec (features, screens, onboarding questions, two end-to-end journeys) lives in the founder's doc "Feta v1 Feature List". Ask the founder to paste a section when you need detail.

The approved designs are in `design/`. Start with `design/README.md` (screens, rules, tokens, motion) and `design/DATA_CONTRACT.md` (data shapes). `design/prototype/Feta App v2.dc.html` is the visual source of truth; `design/explorations/` is earlier rounds for context only (they include dropped ideas such as taste types). Screenshots of every screen and state are in `design/screenshots/light/` and `design/screenshots/dark/`. Prototype content (Lisbon, Tokyo, $ prices, Unsplash photos) is placeholder: real content comes from the user's city and currency (Feta is international from v1). This file and `design/` were checked against each other on 10 Nov 2026 and agree. If you still find a conflict, the design wins for look, copy and flow; this file wins for data, privacy and the rules below. Tell the founder what you found.

## v1 scope (alpha test, international, Nov–Dec 2026)
- International from v1 (founder's decision, 11 Nov 2026; overrides the earlier India-only city list): many alpha testers live abroad. Any city in the world, local currency, km or miles, local booking links and events. Launch cities to test before the alpha: Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Berlin, Frankfurt, Cologne, Koblenz, Prague, Lisbon, Paris, Metz, London, Dubai, Singapore, Hong Kong, Seoul, Melbourne, San Francisco, New York, Houston, Chicago.
- Mobile web app opened from a link. Phone-first, works on slow connections. Light and dark mode: Auto follows the phone, and the user can choose Auto / Light / Dark in You (tokens in `design/README.md`).
- Flow (redesign, founder and Anj's flow, 14 Nov 2026; mock: https://claude.ai/artifact/StSCbvNLSQ4uz5LYjHiw4v): Welcome, three swipeable intro cards that say what Feta is (wording B: "Where should we go tonight? *Ask Feta.*", "Reviews, opening hours, *your budget.*", "YOUR TRAVEL PLANNER · Trips, *planned for you.*"; Skip, ‹ Back, dots and swipes; the 18+ check on the last card) → `/start`: an optional first name ("Hi! What should we call you?", so testers' feedback has a name without sign-in) and "What do you want from Feta?" (Places to eat and drink, Things to do, What to wear, Plan a trip; `lib/profile.ts`, `profiles.first_name` and `wants`) → `/questions`, "Your preferences", at most 4 screens and only what applies to what they want: 1) the basics (diet with "No beef" / "No pork" and Halal only, spend, distance, "Do you drink alcohol?"), 2) "Name a few places you love" (Google suggestions near a city guessed from the time zone, up to 3, saved as Been · Loved it in their own city), 3) "What do you love?" (up to 4 cuisines with "Something else", things to do incl. karaoke, rooftops, board games, dancing, hidden bars, and "Anything else? In your own words"), 4) one vibe card, dressed up and polished vs easy and casual → `/location`, "Last thing: Where should Feta look?" (Use my location, or type a city; an exact area later from Home) → Home, where "Save your taste so Feta remembers you" (Continue with Google / Later) appears once (`components/SaveTaste.tsx`). No recap screen. Who they go out with and the other taste cards are asked later or learned. Old screens (`/sign-in`, `/city`, `/swipes`, `/picked-up`) still work for returns from Google and edits from You.
- Sign-in comes after the first picks (founder, 14 Nov 2026, after testers: asking first loses people): a one-time sheet on Home, and Continue with Google in You. The consent toggles are gone from setup: location consent turns on when they tap "Use my location", public reviews stay off until they turn them on in You. The Welcome screen must not say "No sign-up".
- Consent toggles: "Use my location" and "Show my reviews to others" are both off by default (founder's decision, 10 Nov 2026; this overrides the design, where location is on). Tapping "Use my location" on the Location screen turns the location toggle on. Both can be changed any time in You. The browser's own location prompt still appears only when the user taps "Use my location".
- Diet options: Everything, Vegetarian, Vegetarian + eggs, Jain, Vegan, Pescatarian, plus a Halal only toggle (Jain added by founder's decision).
- Spend per person is shown in the local currency (founder's decision). It comes from the chosen city; if none is chosen yet, it is guessed from the phone's time zone (no permission, not stored; unknown zones fall back to USD). The answer is stored as a level (1–5 or "depends") plus its currency code; the level travels (₹500–1,500 at home means the same kind of place, at local prices, abroad). Bands live in `lib/currency.ts`: hand-set for INR, USD, CAD, GBP, EUR, CHF, CZK, AED, SGD, HKD, THB, AUD, NZD, JPY, KRW; about 40 more scaled from the US bands by a rough rate; anything else falls back to US dollars. India: under ₹300, ₹300–500, ₹500–1,500, ₹1,500–3,000, ₹3,000+. Distances are km, or miles in the US and UK (`formatDistance` in `lib/cities.ts`; the limits stay 1, 2, 5 km).
- No taste types or labels on users. The profile is plain, editable lines, and can hold notes tied to an occasion or a trip.
- Home (`/home`, `components/Home.tsx`, founder's design, 12 Nov 2026): the first tab (tab bar: Home · Lists · cheese · Style · You) and where returning people land. A magazine top (date and area in small capitals, the serif headline "Tonight, *made for you.*" / "This weekend, …" / "Today, …" on a Saturday or Sunday morning, in Fraunces via `--font-fraunces`), Tonight / This weekend, a light/dark switch (`components/ThemeToggle.tsx`: a ☀︎ / ☾ pill with the current look lit, the same Appearance as You; also on the Welcome photo, so the look can be chosen from the first screen), then tiles on one screen (Ask and Style tiles removed 12 Nov 2026: they duplicated the tab bar and Home felt crowded): a wide Food & drinks cover and an Experiences cover showing each category's top pick (photo, name, ★ FETA PICK, "8 picks ›", a round date badge for an event), a black Your evening tile (the best Plan my evening, "4 plans ›", opens the plan sheet; on weekend mornings "Plan my whole day", which opens chat for an itinerary), a pink This weekend tile (ticks and wishes, opens Lists), a yellow Ask tile and a mint Style · coming soon tile. Animations: tiles pop in one after another, cover photos drift, an event badge wobbles in, a sheen while loading, the cheese bobs, tiles squish when tapped (all off with reduced motion). Home and the picks list share one store on the phone (`lib/picks-store.ts`, one request per category even when both screens ask), so Home costs no extra searches. Tapping a cover opens that category's list (`/picks?c=…&w=…`) with a back arrow; the list has no category switch.
- Picks list: "Tonight" and "This weekend" tabs, a city pill, and (before Home) a category switch: "Food & drinks" (EAT and DRINK cards) and "Experiences" (DO and EVENT cards), plus a muted "Style · soon" that opens the Style teaser. Each category shows up to 8 picks, not numbered (founder's decision, 12 Nov 2026; replaces "exactly 3, one card at a time"): a scrolling list with the best fit as a big card (photo, verdict, reason, chips, Google line, ✕ / Details / ♥) and the rest as slim rows (small photo, name, reason, distance and verdict, a short Google Maps line with the photo credit, ♥); tapping a row opens Details, which also has "Not for me". The header stays pinned while the list scrolls: Tonight / This weekend, the city pill, "Tonight's picks for you", the categories, and "⚙ Tweak" (opens the Tweak sheet; no one-tap tweak row, founder's choice) and "✦ Plan my evening". The rest of the ranked places from the same search are kept on the phone as backup: "Show me more" adds the next 8 below at no Google cost, and only searches again once the backup runs out. The two categories are never mixed on this screen. The last category is remembered, each category keeps its own list, and Tweak applies to the category in view. Ask Feta chat still shows the best 3. Chat replies may still mix categories. There is no "Who's with you?" chip (founder's decision): company comes from the onboarding answer, the Tweak sheet ("Better for groups") and chat.
- Every pick has a "Because you…" line, matched chips, honest wording ("Worth a detour" / "Good if you're nearby" / "Skip unless…"), and an action row: the booking or map button, Directions, Save, Send to the group. Booking brands only where they operate (founder's decision): Book on District for tables and BookMyShow / District for events in India only; elsewhere "Open in Google Maps" leads, with the venue's own website, and events link to their ticket page. South Korea: "Open in Naver Map" (a search link by place name) leads, Google Maps second. Mainland China is not supported in v1: choosing a mainland city says so plainly and offers chat-only ideas marked less reliable; Hong Kong and Macau work as normal.
- Chat ("Ask Feta"): plan for a destination (India or abroad), change choices, ask "why this one?". What it learns is added to the profile straight away and shown in an "ADDED TO YOUR PROFILE" card with Undo. When the reply is tied to a trip, the card also has an "Only for this trip" toggle (off by default). Trip-only lines are never used for everyday picks and appear in their own group in the profile, with "Use these every day instead".
- Location in two steps (founder's decision): a "Which city?" screen right after sign-in where the user types any city in the world (no location popup, no "Not yet"; sets currency, units and time zone), then the exact-location screen just before the picks: "Use my location" (one-time, never stored as a trail; the city follows the phone), or a typed neighbourhood or landmark, or "Anywhere in <city>". Cities come from GeoNames (`data/cities.json`, every city of 15,000+ people with English alternative names, built by `scripts/build-cities.mjs`, CC BY 4.0, credited on the privacy page), searched on Feta's server (`lib/server/cities.ts`, `/api/cities`): free, and storable, unlike Google answers. City ids are "gn:<GeoNames id>"; Goa and Bali are added by hand.
- After an outing: Loved it / It was fine / Not for me, optional review and up to 3 photos, with a "Show my review to others" toggle off by default. Photos and public reviews need founder approval on an admin screen.
- Feta Favourite badge: at least 3 ratings and at least 70% loved.
- Style and shopping: a "coming soon" card and tab with a "Tell me when" button that counts interest. No workflow.
- Sign-in provider for the friends test: Google sign-in through Supabase Auth (founder's decision, 10 Nov 2026: free, one tap for most users). This replaces the design's phone number and 6-digit code. Phone codes (Firebase Phone Auth, which handles India's SMS DLT registration) may come back after the trial.
- Weekend plan: drafted by the app, sent by the founder on WhatsApp. No web push in v1. The WhatsApp number is optional and asked separately, not at sign-in: a "Want your weekend plan on WhatsApp?" prompt with a number field, on My lists (founder's decision). It is stored only after the user adds it, used only for the weekend plan, and removable in Lists and You.
- A small feedback button on every screen.
- Not in v1: group mode, full travel mode, native app, background location, in-app booking, allergy filter.

## Changes after the design (founder's decisions, 10 Nov 2026)
These came from a 12-persona test of the flow. They override `design/` wherever the two differ. Keep the design's look and components; change only what is listed.
- Taste cards (founder's decision, 11 Nov 2026; replaces the earlier 6 yes/no swipes): 6 cards, 3 for food and drinks and 3 for experiences. Counter and progress read "n / 6", with a visible "Skip" straight to "Here's what I picked up". 1) Favourite cuisines: a grid, pick up to 4 ("I eat anything" skips; "Local food" and "Indian" lead outside South Asia). Then "This or that?" pairs, tap one photo, or Both / Neither: 2) a hole-in-the-wall with one great dish vs a dressed-up dinner; 3) a quiet café where you can talk vs a buzzing bar with music (a lively café with live music for "No alcohol"); 4) (research, 13 Nov 2026, replacing the old move/make and play/unwind cards) get moving: a dance class, badminton, a hike vs slow down: pottery, a book club, a gallery; 5) (new, research) your usual, done really well vs something you've never tried, which sets how adventurous picks are; 6) in the crowd: a gig, show or match vs in the circle: a run club, trivia night or meetup (ticketed versus community events). No breakfast card: the vibe decides. "What are you into?" still asks what; the cards ask how. Each side is a `swipes` row (chosen "yes", other "no"; cuisines as "cuisine_<id>"), a plain profile line ("Would pick a run club or trivia night over a big gig"), and search words (`CUISINE_PHRASES`, `FOOD_SIDE_PHRASES`, `DOING_SIDE_PHRASES` in `lib/server/picks.ts`). Welcome copy: "A few quick questions, six taste cards, then your picks."
- "New to you" rule for picks: the picks in each category should feel like finds, not the places everyone already knows. At most 2 of the 8 shown may be widely known, very popular places, and not the first. Never show a place the user has saved, marked Been, or skipped. Tell the AI this in the picks prompt; keep diet, budget, distance, alcohol and opening hours as hard filters in code.
- Spend question: add "Under ₹300" as the lowest option (then ₹300–500, ₹500–1,500, ₹1,500–3,000, ₹3,000+, Depends on the day).
- Company question: add "Family with kids". When chosen, picks prefer family-friendly places and the Tweak sheet gains "Good with kids".
- Check-in: users whose diet is Jain, vegan or halal-only see one extra optional tick after "Loved it" or "It was fine": "It worked for my diet (Jain / vegan / halal)". On pick details, show the count as "3 Feta users who eat Jain said it worked for them. Still check with staff." Show it only at 2 or more confirmations. This never replaces the "not confirmed, check with staff" rule and must never say or imply the food is safe.
- Sign-in moved after the first picks (founder, 14 Nov 2026), see Flow.

## Proposed stack (confirm with the founder before installing)
- Next.js web app, hosted on Vercel.
- Supabase for the database, Google sign-in, file storage (review photos) and row-level security.
- AI split (founder's decision, 10 Nov 2026): Venice knows the person, Gemini knows the places.
  - Venice AI (OpenAI-compatible API, founder's credits) for chat, profile updates (with Undo / Only for this trip), turning what the user says into a structured search request, and turning reviews into tags. Venice never receives Google Maps or Places content. Use a mainstream model, not an "uncensored" one.
  - Gemini API with Grounding with Google Maps finds places, ranks the picks per category from those that pass the hard filters, and writes the "Because you…" line and verdict. Gemini receives only a short taste summary built from Feta's own data. Show each pick's Google Maps source right below it, as Google requires.
  - "Why this one?" in chat is answered from the reason Gemini already wrote.
- Google Places API (New) for the hard-filter checks (open now, hours, price level, diet and alcohol signals, distance) and live open-now status on the picks shown.
- Cost guards: a ₹1,000 monthly budget alert in Google Cloud, a daily search limit enforced in Feta, and picks reused per person for a few hours instead of re-searching on every open.
- Web search (Venice or Gemini with Google Search) for this week's events, linking out to the event page.
- Foursquare open places data or Overture Maps as a stored base list of places.
- PostHog for usage analytics.

## How picks work (built 10 Nov 2026)
- Location screen before picks: "Use my location" (one-time, kept only in the tab's session, never stored; turns location consent on; Feta's own city list says which city the point is in), a typed area (Google Places Autocomplete near the city, then the chosen place's location with the cheapest "Essentials" fields; `/api/area`; the phone remembers the area for up to 30 days, as Google allows for a location), or "Anywhere in <city>". Pick sets are keyed by place ID or rounded coordinates, never Google text.
- `/api/picks` (server only, keys never reach phones), one Google Places search per new set. Food & drinks: Nearby Search for every restaurant, café, bakery and bar within the user's distance (founder: "with location on, everything near me should show up"), most popular first, with a new search for "Show me more" (once the backup runs out) skipping what was already shown or kept (closest-first surfaced juice and dessert counters as dinner). Experiences: Text Search with a phrase from the user's interests and swipes, rotating on each new search, never showing places that are mainly for eating or drinking. Then hard filters in code (`lib/server/picks.ts`) → Gemini 3.5 Flash ranks every place that suits them (up to 24) → code shows the first 8 with at most 2 very popular places (3,000+ Google reviews), relaxing that only rather than show fewer, and keeps the rest in order as backup (`spare` in the stored pick) → `pick_sets` stores place IDs and Feta's own text only. Photos are not fetched with the picks: each pick carries a signed photo reference (`lib/server/photo-ref.ts`) and the phone asks `/api/photo` only when the photo comes on screen (`components/PickPhoto.tsx`), so places nobody scrolls to cost nothing. An empty result says which filter removed the places.
- Hard-filter details: places with no price on Google show only for spend levels 1–3 (up to ₹1,500) and "Depends on the day", not for ₹1,500–3,000 or ₹3,000+ (founder's decision); vegetarian diets need Google's "serves vegetarian food" = yes (food and drinks only); "No alcohol" drops bars, pubs and clubs; Tonight, in the city's own time (founder's decision, 11 Nov 2026), means open now from 5 pm to 3 am, and earlier in the day open for at least an hour between 7 and 10 pm today (from weekly hours; the Google line then reads "Open tonight until 23:00"), so daytime and early-morning searches find tonight's places, not lunch spots; This weekend needs Sat or Sun hours; closed businesses, saved, skipped and "been" places are never shown. Halal can't be checked from Google data yet: the option stays (founder's decision, useful outside India), is not used as a filter, and food picks for halal users carry the chip "Halal · not confirmed, check with staff". Add a real halal source before using it as a filter.
- Always 8 when possible: Food & drinks searches everything popular nearby plus a search built from the user's tweaks and "Yes, me" swipes (so picks follow their taste); Experiences searches two interest phrases. If fewer than needed pass the hard filters, one wider search (2× distance, shown as "worth a short ride"). Coming back to the same place within the reuse window runs one fresh search (one search returns 20 places, about half the cost of re-checking 8 one by one) with the same search words, and puts the places shown last time first, in the same order and with the same words, while they still pass the hard filters; events shown last time stay while they're on. Food search words: favourite cuisines ("Local food" is searched as "traditional <country> food"), and at most 2 places of one cuisine (Google's type, e.g. indian_restaurant) among the 8 shown, so favourites are mixed (founder, 12 Nov 2026: Berlin showed 7 Indian of 8).
- Tweaks (`TWEAKS` in `lib/server/picks.ts`): Cheaper (spend one level down), Closer (distance one step down, to 1 km) and Open later (still open at 11 pm) change the hard filters; Quieter, Outdoors, Better for groups, Good with kids and free text steer the search words and Gemini. Tweaked sets always search fresh and are stored apart from the everyday set.
- Picks are reused for 3 hours (Tonight) or 12 hours (weekend), re-checked live with Google each time. Cost limits (founder's decision, 11 Nov 2026, for about 50 alpha testers): `PICKS_DAILY_LIMIT` (new searches a day across everyone, default 600) and `PICKS_USER_DAILY_LIMIT` (per person, default 20; each category counts once), plus `DAILY_BUDGET_INR` (default 170, about ₹5,000 a month): when today's estimated spend reaches it, new searches pause until midnight India time and people see their latest picks. All changeable in Vercel. Every Google and Gemini call is counted per request (`lib/server/costs.ts`, `track` and `withCosts`) into `api_costs` with Google's standard list price; `spend_today_inr()` totals today, leaving out the founder's admin work (routes admin and weekend) so it never pauses testers (cost rows survive "Delete my data", unlinked from the person), and the Monday n8n report gets spend by kind and testers per country. India pricing (about 70% cheaper) needs mostly-India usage, so plan on standard prices. Picks, chat, lists and details routes set `maxDuration` (60 s; details 30 s).
- Matched chips are written by code from verified facts, never by the AI. Jain and vegan chips say "not confirmed, check with staff". Shares to WhatsApp never include diet or chips.
- Honest chips and reasons (persona tests, 14 Nov 2026): the spend chip ("₹1,500–3,000 ✓") shows only for food and drink places whose price is at their level (Google's price range, or a price level at most one below their highest), never for a much cheaper place, a museum or a park; with "Anywhere in <city>" no distance is shown (km from the city's centre point meant nothing); a "dressed-up" reason needs fine dining or an expensive place, a "hole-in-the-wall" reason a place that isn't expensive (`SIDE_PRICE` in `reasonFits`).
- Experiences = venues + this week's events (founder's decision: A+B). Events: Gemini 3.5 Flash with Grounding with Google Search, via the Interactions API (`lib/server/events.ts`; generateContent with a JSON schema returned no search sources, so it may invent events). Sent: the area name (never coordinates) and the taste summary; at most 2 searches, low thinking, 18 s cap, then places only. Code checks up to 4 events (each venue lookup costs about ₹3; at most 2 are shown): a real https listing link (no redirects), the right date and time in the city's time zone, the budget (unknown price follows the no-price rule), and distance via the venue found on Google Maps (cheaper "Pro" fields). At most 2 events per set, then venues to make 3; venue ranking runs alongside the event search. Event cards are EVENT, link out ("Tickets on BookMyShow"), and show Google's Search Suggestions chip unaltered where food cards show the Google Maps line (Google requires it). Event listings are kept with the set; for an event, place_id is "event:<page address>".
- Lists (`/lists`, `/api/lists`): Want to try (saved) and Been, from `pick_actions` (place IDs only; names and photos fetched live, photos only for the top 8). Each place has a private note; "I've been" moves it to Been; Remove deletes the row. The optional WhatsApp number for the weekend plan lives at the bottom of Lists (logged in `consent_log` as "whatsapp").
- You (`/you`, founder's ask, 11 Nov 2026: "What Feta knows about you"): everyday lines grouped Food & drinks / Things to do / How you go out (by `line_key`); lines from answers and taste cards are changed by "Change my answers" or "Redo taste cards" (both return to You), lines from chat, tweaks or typed here can be edited; "Add something about you" (source "you"). People you go out with: lines keyed `people:<partner|family|friends|kids>:…`, from chat (Venice tags "about") or added here with ＋ Partner / Family / Friends / Kids; they reach Venice and Gemini through the taste summary. Trip-only groups with "Use these every day instead". Your places: counts of to-try and been, linking to Lists. Settings and privacy folded at the bottom: Continue with Google for anonymous users (returns to You; if that Google account already has a Feta account, it signs in to it without deleting this phone's anonymous data), Appearance Auto / Light / Dark (localStorage `feta.theme`, applied before paint), location and public-review consent toggles (logged), the WhatsApp number with Remove, privacy policy, and Delete my data with inline confirm (full reload after).
- Ask Feta (`/ask`, `/api/ask`), opened from the cheese: Venice (`claude-haiku-5-5`, a mainstream model, JSON replies, `lib/server/venice.ts`) reads the chat (last 12 messages, ordered by id) and a taste summary, and returns a short reply, tap-able answers when it asks a question (shown in place of the usual prompts), up to 3 lines to remember, an optional trip, an optional search, and "why". Learned lines go straight into `profile_lines` (source chat) and show in an ADDED TO YOUR PROFILE card with Undo and, for trips, "Only for this trip" (sets `trip`). A search runs through the same picks engine (`lib/server/engine.ts`, shared with `/api/picks`, called directly, not over HTTP): any city in the world by name (Feta's own list), or a neighbourhood, landmark or region found on Google Maps and placed in its nearest city (an area in the person's own city, otherwise the whole place as a trip). Mainland China gets ideas from Venice's own knowledge, marked less reliable, and no search. The person's own words lead the place search. "Why this one?" answers from Feta's stored reasons without searching. For a plan that matters (date night, birthday, family visiting, a trip) it asks first, one question at a time and at most 3, skipping what it already knows and remembering what it learns about the people ("Partner loves live music"); quick everyday asks and "surprise me" search straight away (founder, 11 Nov 2026). Chat history keeps text, place IDs and Feta's words only.
- Feedback pill (`components/Feedback.tsx`, in the layout on every screen): message, optional screenshot of the screen behind (html-to-image, small JPEG, the sheet itself left out), saved to the `feedback` table (insert-only for users; the founder reads it on /admin → Feedback, newest first, with the screen, phone type and screenshot). A daily n8n workflow on her Mac copies new feedback into a Google Sheet through `feedback_since(report_key, since)` (same report key as the weekly report; message, screen, phone, screenshot yes/no, never names), with Gemini tagging Bug / Idea / Confusing / Praise.
- Location on return (friends' feedback, 12 Nov 2026): "Use my location" lasts only for the visit, so reopening Feta fell back to the whole city and measured distances from its centre. Home now asks the phone for its location again, quietly and once per visit, when the browser already allows it (`lib/near-me.ts`; never stored, no prompt), before loading picks; the area line reads "Near you", the chosen area, or "All of <city>". Directions links use exact coordinates with the place ID (a name alone opened other places), cards show Google's stars ("★ 4.5 (1.2k)") with the Google Maps credit, Lists shows "Been ✓" without a date (marking it doesn't say when they went), and chat searches always use the person's own words, so they never get the last list again.
- Persona and friends' review fixes (12 Nov 2026): Vegetarian, Jain and vegan users ("Option B", founder's decision): besides Google's "serves vegetarian food", places that are mainly meat or fish are dropped, by type and name (`mainlyNonVeg` in `lib/server/picks.ts`: steakhouse, seafood, barbecue, biryani, kebab…) and by Gemini, which flags them in "nonVeg" and ranks veg-friendly places first; never a "pure veg" claim. What to order follows their diet and drinks (no fish for vegetarians, no cocktails or tiramisu for no alcohol), and for halal or Jain users the same Gemini call reports what reviews say ("Reviews mention halal-certified meat"), shown in Details with "Not confirmed, check with staff" and no extra cost. Gemini sees the this-or-that choices as "Chose" and "Said no to"; a reason that leans on a side they turned down is replaced in code (`reasonFits`) with one that fits the place (its cuisine, or the kind of place). Each favourite cuisine found by the search is swapped into the 8 (at most 2 of one cuisine). Round 2 (testers: 7 of 8 Italian): the food search looks for all favourite cuisines at once with one Nearby Search by place type (`tastePlaceTypes`, `CUISINE_PLACE_TYPES`: Italian and pizza, Japanese, sushi and ramen…, plus bars for a buzzing-bar pick and cafés for a quiet café, and vegetarian restaurants for vegetarians), alongside one text search for their words or a card's phrase (or what's popular nearby); the cap counts pizza as Italian and so on (`CUISINE_GROUPS`) and holds while filling, with a third of one cuisine only as the very last resort. ## Rules that must not be broken
- Diet, budget, distance, alcohol and opening hours are hard filters enforced in code, never left to the AI.
- Never claim food is safe for an allergy or diet. Jain and vegan matches say "not confirmed, check with staff". Use neutral diet labels; never "pure veg".
- Google data: store only Google place IDs. Show live Google data on its own labelled line, e.g. "Open now · checked just now · Google Maps", with the words "Google Maps" unchanged. Never use Google content to train or test AI models.
- Do not scrape Zomato, District, Swiggy, BookMyShow or Meetup. Link out to them.
- Published lists (Condé Nast Traveller, 50 Best, Culinary Culture, 30 Best Bars) may be used only as "Featured in" facts with links. Never copy their text, photos or logos.
- Privacy: 18+ check, itemised consent with a separate location toggle, a consent log, one-tap "delete my data", a grievance email. Nothing public by default. Diet never appears on anything shareable.
- Every place, event and user record carries city, country, time zone and currency, so new cities are a data job, not a rebuild.
